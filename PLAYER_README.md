# Goblins of the Ashborn Isles 0.6.0

Giltfang joins the Ashborn as the sixth crown. Its northern main island and
Tolltooth companion have a combined land area of 89.97% of Brackmaw, with nine
new coastal sea tiles connected to the existing Atlantic network. Chainhaven
starts with its own market; Hooktooth keeps the southern market. Every goblin
crown begins knowing all Ashborn land and surrounding coastal tiles.

Vrekk the Brass-Tooth leads the Cinderweight from the Crown of Weights and
Chains. Giltfang has 22 districts, its own court, flag, portraits, opening story
and three projects, plus sacred sites at Chainhaven and Tolltooth. All six
crowns participate in the Gathering, Eastern Hunger, Harbor Bargains,
Compact talks, Covenant stories and the separate Ashborn Voyages situation.

Harbor descriptions now wrap into short lines. Project buttons use short
labels and show highlighted native costs and rewards on hover. Requirements
use readable conditions, broken punctuation is repaired, and both parties
receive signing receipts listing the actual payment, benefit, burden and term.
Earlier portrait, wardrobe, hair, Drogg and holy-site fixes are retained.

Clan flags explicitly select their authored emblems. A one-time monthly repair
also corrects saved placeholder flags, including Giltfang's blue circle on yellow.
Its intended flag is a brass fang and chain links on dark blue. To repair an
existing 0.6.0 campaign, install with EU5 closed, restart, load your save and
advance to the next month. This flag repair alone needs no new campaign.

Starting projects now cost **25 gold**, a quarter of the 100-gold opening
treasury; the AI keeps a 20-gold reserve. Food rewards explicitly raise
the **province food storage limit**, with tooltip guidance to the native
stockpile display and its modifier breakdown. They do not create food
or increase monthly production.

Lantern Cay is a new three-district Cindermaw outpost beside an ordinary
coastal passage between the northern and southern islands. The lower
eastern impassable strip is open, and parts of three nearby current
tiles become coastal water. The route does not require fast-current
tiles. The Isles now contain nine islands, 97 districts and 28 coastal
sea tiles; Cindermaw gains 18,000 people on the new cay.

Use a NEW campaign: the world map, country roster and markets have changed.
Install with EU5 closed. Static checks cover geography, native definitions,
all-six-crown event scenarios, terrain reconstruction and package hashes.
In-game sailing, market trade, UI layout and rendering remain playtest checks.
This is local 0.6.0 staging work, separate from GitHub releases and Workshop.

## Install

Extract this archive into a new writable folder. Close EU5 and run
Install-Goblins.cmd. Enable one Goblins copy in the launcher and start a fresh
1337 campaign. The installer checks and reconstructs terrain from your installed
EU5 files, then backs up the prior local mod. Allow roughly 4 GB of working space.
Target game version: Europa Universalis V 1.3.11.

## Playing the Isles

All six crowns start with 100 gold. Each opening offers three mutually exclusive
25-gold projects lasting five years, plus a free decline. Hover over a choice to
see its effects. AI crowns keep a 20-gold reserve for funded projects.

To inspect a food storage reward, select one of your locations and hover
the province food display. Within that tooltip, hover the stockpile total
to see stored food / maximum food and the capacity modifier breakdown.

Unification needs all 97 homeland districts within one independent crown's
realm through direct ownership, qualifying vassals, Compact charters or junior
union partners. Allies and tributaries do not count.

Harbor Bargains cost 10 gold, paid only at signing. A counteroffer changes the
service and costs 15. Provisions give the buyer +10% province food storage
limit and commit 5% of the supplier's limit; pilots give +5% naval morale
recovery and commit 2.5% of the supplier's naval morale recovery. Both last five years. Each crown can hold one contract.
Both signatories receive a receipt describing the actual terms. Hostility or
annexation ends commitments at the next monthly check without a refund.

Compact talks require a completed five-year Harbor Bargain with that partner,
three observed years of alliance, sufficient opinion, strength and rank.
Charters require both parties' consent and create native subject relations.

Ashborn Voyages becomes available independently of unification. Initial
preparation takes 36 months. East costs 5 gold and takes four months; north and
south each cost 10 and take six months. Crews rest for 12 months after a voyage.
Use Review Ashborn Voyages in its own situation to reopen routes; reminders
can be postponed or stopped. Foreign harbor owners discover the Isles on return.

Two separate markets and shared homeland discovery enable inter-island trading;
actual trades still depend on EU5's prices, goods, merchant capacity and AI.

See LORE.md for each crown and TESTING.md for the gameplay acceptance checks.
