0.5.4 - Districts and Dynasties

- Expanded to 72 inhabited locations and 30 provinces.
- Increased population to 1,294,755; larger minor clans, with Shatterfin at 213,487.
- Expanded buildings and once-only RGO investment; added valuable and supporting resources.
- Restricted Ironfang succession to the ruling dynasty; country names now follow the reigning house.
- Varied rulers aged 27–39 and family/court birthdays, with adult dynastic heirs.
- Added +15 years of Ashborn life expectancy, excluding humans.
- Preserved the 0.5.3 portrait, infantry, recruitment art and first-contact work.

Requires EU5 1.3.11 and a new 1337 campaign.
