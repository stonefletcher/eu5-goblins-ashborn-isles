[h1]Goblins of the Ashborn Isles[/h1]
[b]Version 0.6.0 | Europa Universalis V 1.3.11 | New 1337 campaign required[/b]

Fire rose from the Atlantic. When the smoke cleared, goblin crowns claimed the new islands between the Azores and Portugal. Lead one of six rival nations, build your harbors, bargain with neighboring crowns and chart foreign shores.

[h2]New in 0.6.0[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Giltfang, the sixth crown:[/b] Vrekk the Brass-Tooth rules Chainhaven and Tolltooth. The new realm is about 10% smaller than Brackmaw, with 22 districts, its own court, culture, lore, flag and opening artwork.
[*][b]Two markets:[/b] Chainhaven has its own market beside Hooktooth's southern market. All six goblin crowns begin knowing the homeland and surrounding waters.
[*][b]Lantern Cay and the Quiet Road:[/b] Cindermaw gains a three-district outpost. Reworked sea tiles and an open eastern approach connect north and south through ordinary coastal water without requiring fast-current tiles.
[*][b]Six crowns throughout:[/b] Giltfang participates in shared situations, events, bargains, Compact charters, religion and unification.
[*][b]Clearer choices:[/b] Shorter prompts, highlighted costs and rewards on hover, readable requirements, repaired text and signed-pact receipts explaining both partners' commitments.
[*][b]Ashborn Voyages:[/b] A separate situation for expedition preparation, sailing, arrival, rest and charted routes.
[*][b]Starting projects:[/b] Three projects per clan now cost 25 gold from a 100-gold treasury. Food bonuses explain province storage capacity rather than implying food production.
[*][b]Correct clan flags:[/b] Explicit emblem assignments and a one-time monthly repair for placeholders saved in an existing 0.6.0 campaign.
[/list]

[h2]Six clans, nine islands[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Cindermaw / Emberblood:[/b] Drogg the Stone Fletcher rules the forge city of Hooktooth and the largest island realm.
[*][b]Brackmaw / Brineward:[/b] Marshes, storehouses and the rival crown of Brackhaven.
[*][b]Reefhook / Reefstrider:[/b] Shoals, fisheries and a seafaring court.
[*][b]Shatterfin / Stormfang:[/b] The Tidemothers and a ruling house following maternal seniority.
[*][b]Sootwake / Ashveil:[/b] Woodland crafts, charcoal and quiet uplands.
[*][b]Giltfang / Cinderweight:[/b] Copper, salt, honest weights and armed harbor crews under Vrekk the Brass-Tooth.
[/list]

The archipelago contains 97 districts, 28 coastal sea tiles, 75 ports and twelve sacred sites. Gather the crowns through conquest, vassalage or senior unions. Negotiate Harbor Bargains and Compact charters, follow Ashen Covenant rites and stories, and send voyages toward Iberia, northern waters and northwest Africa. The Eastern Hunger supports a European foothold through normal EU5 warfare.

[h2]Installation and saves[/h2]
Enable only one Goblins copy and disable the old Gathering Prototype add-on. Restart EU5 and start a [b]new 1337 campaign[/b] when upgrading from 0.5.x: the world map and starting setup have changed. Existing 0.6.0 saves can receive the flag repair after restarting and advancing to the next month.

Other map, terrain or starting-world mods may conflict. Keep separate saves.

[h2]Development status[/h2]
Static map, script, artwork, archive and isolated-installation checks passed. Sailing, actual trade, UI rendering, flag appearance and save/reload behavior still need gameplay confirmation. Multiplayer and achievements are unverified. Dedicated foreign invasion ambitions and fear mechanics are not included.

Previous portrait, skin, hairstyle, Drogg, wardrobe and holy-site fixes are retained. Report issues with your version, clan, enabled mods and reproduction steps; screenshots and saves are welcome.
